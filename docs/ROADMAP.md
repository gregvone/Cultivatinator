# Roadmap

1. **Application Foundation** — current implementation: shell, empty dashboard/API, SQLite/Alembic, Docker storage/deployment, tests and docs.
2. **Culture Library and Genetic Lineage** — biological identities, permanent identifiers, ancestry relationships and histories; interactive lineage visualization in a later increment.
3. **Inventory and Reusable Recipes** — materials, equipment, locations, purchase costs and precise transactional quantities.
4. **Laboratory Management** — growing cultures, agar batches, individual plates, liquid cultures, germination, transfers and observations.
5. **Spawn Production** — preparation, grain, jars/bags and colonization.
6. **Fruiting and Harvest Tracking** — growing units, flushes, harvests, cloning and spore collection links.
7. **Dashboard Enhancements, Environmental Tracking, and Reporting** — database aggregates, attention items, timelines, manual conditions, yield/performance statistics. Sensor integrations are optional future work.

Photographs and complete downloadable backup/restore need dedicated increments before users rely on UI recovery. Future milestone details remain subject to design approval; this roadmap does not authorize implementing them in Milestone 1.
