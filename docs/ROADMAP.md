# Roadmap

1. **Foundation, Culture Library 2A and Provenance 2B — implemented.** SQLite/Alembic, Docker persistence, reference strains, acquisitions, individual cultures, permanent IDs, photographs, history, archive/restore, genetic origins, clones, isolates, spore establishments and shared/specific grow provenance.
2. **2C.1 — implemented.** Dashboard Quick Create, material-specific source selection, Create From This and context-preserving legacy redirects. See [2C.1](MILESTONE_2C1.md).
3. **2C.2 — implemented, under functional review.** Four Recipes categories (LC Media, Agar, Spawn Seed, Substrate); designated default LC media and inherited syringe media; direct cultivation without equipment, purchases, empty-container stock or ingredient accounting. Automatic physical labels are distinct from permanent biological IDs, with reusable container occupancy/history, explicit agar/spawn readiness and one-operation agar consumption. Acquisitions receives outside material; Culture Library owns Add Strain. Measured LC withdrawals and existing preparation/accounting records remain compatible. See [2C.2](MILESTONE_2C2.md) and [label/lifecycle rules](CONTAINER_LABELS.md).
4. **2C.3 — next, practical fruiting workflow.** Select Ready spawn, choose a Substrate recipe, specify substrate amount and optionally spawn amount/ratio, then atomically create one or more permanent FR records with physical labels. Mark consumed spawn Used exactly once and release reusable J labels, retaining exact source allocations. Track fruiting date and first harvest date. Record flush number, harvest date and fresh harvest weight. Explicit completion/failure releases reusable F labels; a harvest or flush does not. Require only information that materially helps evaluate cultivation results. Manual 2B provenance registration remains separate and does not simulate consumption. No mandatory prepared-substrate inventory or equipment counts.
5. **Later — Basic Performance.** Calculate success/failure rate, average colonization time, days to first harvest and total harvest weight by strain, culture line and recipe. Show sample counts alongside averages. No daily logs required; observations may record meaningful changes in fresh air exchange, humidity, temperature or contamination.
6. **Later — Experimental Genetics.** Preserve clones, isolates, spore-derived cultures, candidate crosses and confirmed crosses. Keep potential parents separate from confirmed genetic parents. Mixing strains is never evidence of a successful cross. No elaborate breeding module in 2C.2.

Existing permanent IDs, genetic genealogy, physical provenance, photographs, recipe snapshots, acquisitions, archive/history and LC volume accounting must remain intact. Archiving is a visibility action and never releases an occupied physical label. Downloadable backup/restore and optional sensors remain future work. No purchase, equipment inventory or preparation setup is required by the practical cultivation workflow.
